## 1.Project Overview

Typing Tutor is a desktop application designed to help users improve their typing speed and accuracy. It will have two main sections: Learning and Practice.

The Learning section will teach touch typing, correct hand placement, finger positions, and keyboard use through lessons. The Practice section will allow users to practise independently through timed tests, free practice, and accuracy-focused exercises.

The application will provide an interactive keyboard, real-time character feedback, performance reports, saved progress, mistake analysis, optional recommended exercises, and a leaderboard showing the top 20 users.

## 2.Problem Statement

Many people struggle with typing speed, accuracy, and correct finger placement. Beginners may also find it difficult to identify their mistakes and track improvement. Typing Tutor aims to solve these problems by providing guided lessons, independent practice, instant feedback, and progress reports in one application.

## 3.Project Objectives

- Teach users correct touch-typing techniques and finger placement.
- Provide an interactive keyboard and progressive typing lessons.
- Allow users to practise without completing lessons first.
- Show correct and incorrect characters in different colours.
- Calculate typing speed, accuracy, errors, and time taken.
- Save users' learning progress, practice history, and best scores.
- Identify common mistakes and offer optional targeted exercises.
- Display a leaderboard of the top 20 users.

## 4.Intended Users

The application is intended for anyone who wants to improve their typing skills, including beginners, students, office workers, and regular computer users. It will support different skill levels through lessons and practice options.
